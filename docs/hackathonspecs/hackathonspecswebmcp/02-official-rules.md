## 4. How To Enter

1. Register on the Hackathon Website (webmcp.devpost.com) via "Join Hackathon" — requires a free Devpost account.
2. Obtain access to required developer tools/platforms (subject to their own license agreements). Entering constitutes consent for Sponsor/Devpost to collect and use your personal information to operate/publicize the Hackathon.
3. **Get access to WebMCP:** download the ChatGPT desktop app (its in-app browser supports WebMCP by default), OR download Google Chrome 149+ and enable `chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing`, then restart.
4. Make sure judges can access your working live project via ChatGPT's in-app browser or Google Chrome with WebMCP enabled.
5. Optionally install the Devpost Plugin from the Hackathon Website (not required — see Section 5 below).
6. Optionally, request **3,000 free Netlify credits** while supplies last: complete https://forms.gle/xw75XGUQzCXEiALc7 by **September 1, 12:00 pm PT**. Only registered Entrants can request. Credits are non-cash and must be redeemed by **October 3, 2026**.
7. Complete and submit all required fields on the "Enter a Submission" page during the Submission Period.

### Project Requirements

- **What to Create:** A WebMCP-powered web app that imagines and explores the future of the open web — where humans and agents can interact, collaborate, and create together.
- **Functionality:** Must successfully install/run consistently on its intended platform and function as depicted in the video/description.
- **Platforms:** Must run on the platform specified in the Submission.
- **New & Existing:** Projects must be newly created during the Submission Period, **or**, if pre-existing, must be **meaningfully extended using WebMCP after the Submission Period start date**. Pre-existing projects are evaluated only on work added during the Submission Period. Entrants with pre-existing projects must document what's new vs. prior work — e.g., timestamped/dated commit history.
- **Third-Party Integrations:** Must be authorized to use any third-party SDKs/APIs/data per their own terms.

### Submission Requirements

- A **working live URL** accessible via ChatGPT's in-app browser or Google Chrome with WebMCP enabled. Host anywhere: ChatGPT Sites, Cloudflare, Vercel, Render, Netlify, or any other provider. Authentication is allowed if credentials are provided on the Submission Form.
- A **text description** explaining:
  - Why your use case is a strong fit for WebMCP
  - How it creates a better user experience
  - What people and agents can do together that was difficult or impossible before
  - A brief explanation of how you implemented WebMCP
- A **public code repository URL** (GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket) that must:
  - Contain all necessary source code, assets, and instructions for the project to be functional
  - Be open source with a detectable, visible open-source license file at the top of the repo page (in the "About" section)
- A **demonstration video**, which:
  - Must be **under 3 minutes** (judges are not required to watch beyond that)
  - Must clearly demo the project functioning, with audio covering what you built and how you used WebMCP
  - Must be uploaded to and publicly visible on **YouTube**, linked from the submission form
  - Must not include third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material without permission

### Multiple Submissions

An Entrant may not submit more than one Submission; however, each Submission must be unique and substantially different from the Entrant's other Submissions, as determined by Sponsor/Devpost in their sole discretion.

Must be the Entrant's original work, solely owned by the Entrant, and not violate any third party's IP rights.

## 7. Judges & Criteria

Sponsor/Administrator have sole discretion over eligibility and judging methodology (expert panels, peer review, automated AI-driven analysis, or combinations). Judges may be Sponsor employees or third parties, may or may not be individually listed, and may change during Judging.

**Stage One (pass/fail):** Does the idea meet a baseline level of viability — does the Project reasonably fit the theme and reasonably apply the required APIs/SDKs?

**Stage Two (equally weighted criteria, sole and absolute discretion of judges):**
1. WebMCP Leverage
2. Execution
3. Potential Impact
4. Creativity & Ambition

*(Full descriptions in `01-overview.md`.)*

**Tie Breaking:** Ties broken by highest score on criterion #1 (WebMCP Leverage) first, then #2, etc. If still tied on all criteria, the panel of Judges votes.
