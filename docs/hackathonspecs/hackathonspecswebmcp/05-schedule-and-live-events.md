## Live events — NOT listed anywhere on Devpost

These two events appear only on **OpenAI's own landing page** (https://openai.com/webmcp-challenge/) and are corroborated by **Netlify's blog post** and an **OpenAI Discord/X announcement** — someone relying on Devpost alone would miss them entirely.

### Opening livestream
- **When:** August 25, 2026, 3:00 pm PT
- **Where:** X (Twitter) broadcast — https://x.com/i/broadcasts/1qGoNYbWdkvKv
- **What:** Per an X post from an OpenAI team member: *"Today, we are adding support for WebMCP in the ChatGPT desktop in-app browser, and kicking off a hackathon to encourage builders to build agent-native websites... We'll be doing a livestream at 3pm today on @OpenAIDevs to talk through this future, WebMCP and the hackathon."*
- Netlify's blog independently confirms this same time as "kickoff livestream with example apps and implementation ideas."

### Office hours
- **When:** August 31, 2026, 11:00 am PT
- **Where:** OpenAI Discord — https://discord.com/channels/974519864045756446/1517482456964534312
- **What:** Community office hours. Render's own promotional post independently confirms Render-specific office hours on the same date ("Office hours 8/31, 11am PT").
- Netlify's blog describes this as "community office hours with our team" (i.e., Netlify's team specifically) — it's plausible this is a shared, multi-sponsor office-hours slot rather than one company's event; treat it as the one general office-hours window to plan around.

## Practical planning note

The entire building window is genuinely **10 days** (Aug 25 – Sep 3), matching the Devpost tagline. If you want to attend both live events, block:
- Aug 25, 3:00 pm PT (kickoff/context, likely worth attending on day one before you've committed to an idea)
- Aug 31, 11:00 am PT (roughly the halfway point — good time to get unstuck)
