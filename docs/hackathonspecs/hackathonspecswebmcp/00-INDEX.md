# WebMCP Challenge — Spec Folder Index

This folder contains everything gathered about **The WebMCP Challenge**, a hackathon hosted on Devpost and run by OpenAI with Cloudflare, Vercel, Shopify, Google Chrome, Render, and Netlify as supporting sponsors.

- **Hackathon hub (Devpost):** https://webmcp.devpost.com/
- **Official OpenAI landing page:** https://openai.com/webmcp-challenge/
- **Submission deadline:** September 3, 2026 @ 1:00 PM PDT
- **Prize pool:** $35,000+ across 10 winning teams
- **Research conducted:** August 26, 2026 (the hackathon was in its first 24–36 hours; participant counts and any Updates/Discussions content will have grown since)

## How this was compiled

Every Devpost tab was fetched directly (Overview, Rules, Resources, Details/Dates, Updates, Project Gallery, Discussions, and both open forum threads). From there, every external resource linked *from* Devpost was also fetched: the W3C WebMCP spec, all of Chrome's `developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/webmcp/*` sub-pages, OpenAI's `learn.chatgpt.com` WebMCP doc, Shopify's `shopify.dev/docs/api/web-mcp`, Cloudflare's WebMCP launch blog, and the OpenAI Showcase. Finally, a general web search surfaced the official `openai.com/webmcp-challenge` landing page and Netlify's own challenge blog post, both of which contain details (exact livestream time, office-hours time, per-sponsor credit mechanics) that are **not** printed anywhere on the Devpost pages.

## A note on freshness

This is a very new, very fast-moving 10-day hackathon (it opened August 25, 2026 and this research was done August 26, 2026). Two things will change and are **not** captured live by these files:
1. The **Updates** tab on Devpost was empty at research time — organizers said they'd post announcements there.
2. The **Discussions** tab had exactly two threads, both with 0 replies, asking legitimate clarification questions (see `09-discussions-and-open-questions.md`). Check back on Devpost directly for official answers.
